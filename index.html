Why Schools Write "Bad" Goals

Schools are overwhelmed. They often use vague template language to minimize their legal liability. This leaves your child without the specific support they are entitled to under federal law (IDEA).

The "Weasel Word" Trap

Phrases like "will try" or "opportunities to" measure effort, not outcome. If a goal isn't objective, you can't prove the school failed to teach it.

The "Endrew F." Standard

In 2017, the Supreme Court ruled that IEPs must be "reasonably calculated to enable a child to make progress." Minimal progress is no longer enough.

SMART Goals Win

We rewrite goals to be Specific, Measurable, Attainable, Relevant, and Time-bound. This creates clarity and accountability.
